> **Objective:** Define a low-ceremony way to explore, review and transition design into execution.

## How to complete this page

Write in English. Replace prompts with actual findings; keep unresolved questions visible. Use short paragraphs, tables and diagrams only when they improve understanding. Label evidence, assumptions and proposals separately.

## Design rhythm

**Required work:** Start with purpose, examples and questions; limit active elements to realistic capacity.

**Expected output / writing format:** Weekly focus / active pages / review plan.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Research workflow

**Required work:** Discover question, assess impact, investigate with contributors, decide or keep open, update design.

**Expected output / writing format:** Origin / question / note / outcome / design update.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Review & decision

**Required work:** Review proportionally; record approval scope and evidence.

**Expected output / writing format:** Page / review criteria / reviewer / agreed scope.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Jira transition

**Required work:** Create or link Jira work only when scope and expected deliverable are clear, or when tracking an explicit research spike is useful.

**Expected output / writing format:** Work package / design link / acceptance criteria / Jira link.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Page status and question impact

| Design status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not Started | Scaffold; no study yet |
| Exploring | Understanding and questions being developed |
| In Review | Proposed understanding ready for qualified review |
| Agreed | Reviewed design within recorded scope; not implemented |
| Needs Revision | New evidence or conflict warrants revision |

Questions: Blocking (only affected scope stops), Non-blocking (proceed with explicit assumption), Deferred (outside current scope). Record actual impact independently of design status. Research statuses and decision statuses belong in their respective records.

## Change rule

Stable IDs are independent of numbering. Rename a title without changing its ID; move a page by updating the parent links and architecture map; preserve a redirect or superseded note when merging or replacing. Run link validation after changes. Avoid manually duplicating index data; rebuild the ownership table from the page headers as needed.

## Review rule

Every material design slice needs a different-person reviewer. Consult affected component owners. Mark Agreed only when scope, expected output and open-blocker disposition are documented; revise later if evidence changes. No daily documentation ceremony is required.
