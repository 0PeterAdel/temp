## How to complete this page

Write in English. Replace prompts with actual findings; keep unresolved questions visible. Use short paragraphs, tables and diagrams only when they improve understanding. Label evidence, assumptions and proposals separately.

## Actors & goals

**Required work:** Identify the users, external agents and other actors; distinguish their goals from platform responsibilities.

**Expected output / writing format:** Actor / goal / permissions or constraints.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Normal journeys

**Required work:** Write at least two contrasting candidate tasks with inputs, expected outcomes and lifecycle behavior.

**Expected output / writing format:** Scenario ID / actor / preconditions / steps / expected result.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Alternative & failure journeys

**Required work:** Show ambiguity, blocked permissions, failed execution and failed verification without assuming all are implemented.

**Expected output / writing format:** Trigger / expected behavior / responsible component.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Scenario coverage

**Required work:** Map scenarios to requirements and architecture questions.

**Expected output / writing format:** Scenario / requirement IDs / components / evidence needed.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._
