> **Objective:** Record material design decisions with accountable authority, rationale, evidence and affected components.

## How to complete this page

Write in English. Replace prompts with actual findings; keep unresolved questions visible. Use short paragraphs, tables and diagrams only when they improve understanding. Label evidence, assumptions and proposals separately.

## Decision register

**Required work:** Record open, proposed, agreed, rejected or superseded decisions; one row per material decision.

**Expected output / writing format:** ID / question / status / authority / affected components / canonical decision.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Decision detail

**Required work:** Use a decision note for significant trade-offs; link evidence, alternatives, scoped approval and revisit triggers.

**Expected output / writing format:** Decision / alternatives / rationale / consequences / approval / review.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Propagation

**Required work:** Update affected design pages and link any approved ADR in the real repository.

**Expected output / writing format:** Decision / affected IDs / update required / ADR link.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Review checklist

- [ ] All required outputs above are present or explicitly marked not applicable with a reason.
- [ ] Claims are supported; proposed material and unknowns are marked.
- [ ] A different person reviews the page, including relevant domain specialists if needed.
- [ ] Links to impacted design, research and decisions are current.
