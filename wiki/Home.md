## First working session

1. Confirm the seven-seat role mapping and actual capacity.
2. Read the product scope and architecture map; agree which pages to explore first.
3. Assign one owner, contributors and a different-person reviewer per active page.
4. Pilot a component (e.g. Task Execution); fill only the elements needed now.
5. Record questions where discovered; open deeper research when necessary and link findings back.
6. Review the design slice; later create linked Jira work when ready.

## Where things belong

Wiki: evolving concept, research and rationale. Real product repository: code, approved technical specifications, ADRs and versioned reproducible evidence. Jira: delivery work and actual ticket state. Academic source files remain in academic storage. No prototype repository is used as authority.

## Reading and editing

English for official content. Use short clear explanations and concrete examples. Keep one canonical question and research note; link rather than duplicate. Do not treat prompts or starter examples as approved conclusions.
