> **Objective:** Maintain one research note per meaningful investigation; any discipline may participate.

## How to complete this page

Write in English. Replace prompts with actual findings; keep unresolved questions visible. Use short paragraphs, tables and diagrams only when they improve understanding. Label evidence, assumptions and proposals separately.

## Active research

**Required work:** Link investigation notes, identify one research lead, contributors and independent reviewer; do not require a note for every small question.

**Expected output / writing format:** Research ID / question / origin / role / status / note.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Completed findings

**Required work:** Link concluded and inconclusive investigations and state the design impact briefly.

**Expected output / writing format:** Research ID / finding / limitation / affected design.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Source quality

**Required work:** Require primary sources where possible, dates, applicability and experimental evidence; distinguish observation from interpretation.

**Expected output / writing format:** Source / supported claim / limitations.

**Working content:** _To be developed by the owner and contributors._

## Review checklist

- [ ] All required outputs above are present or explicitly marked not applicable with a reason.
- [ ] Claims are supported; proposed material and unknowns are marked.
- [ ] A different person reviews the page, including relevant domain specialists if needed.
- [ ] Links to impacted design, research and decisions are current.
