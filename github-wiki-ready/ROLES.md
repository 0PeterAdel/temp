## Role charter — proposed baseline

| Role | Accountability | Boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Product Owner | Product goal, value, scope, priority and stakeholder communication | Does not unilaterally choose technical implementation |
| Scrum Master | Team effectiveness, facilitation, impediments and process improvement | Not a line manager or task dispatcher |
| Technical Lead / Software Architect | Coordinate architecture consistency, integration and technical review | Consult affected disciplines; no self-approval |
| AI Engineer & Research Lead | Cognitive runtime, agents, planning, memory, research methodology | Research participation is open to all roles |
| Backend Lead | Backend boundaries, APIs, lifecycle and contracts | Coordinate with architecture, security and data owners |
| Backend Engineer (Data & Persistence Owner) | Data, state, consistency and integration | Consult specialists on trust and AI semantics |
| Cybersecurity & Backend Engineer (Security Owner) | Threats, permissions, identity, trust and isolation | Work with DevOps and engineering owners |
| DevOps Engineer (Infrastructure Owner) | Environments, CI/CD, observability and deployment | SM only if separately agreed |
| Frontend Engineer (Frontend Owner) | Client architecture, integration and UI behavior | User experience co-designed with UX |
| UI/UX Designer & Mobile (Design Owner) | User research, journeys, design and prototypes | Coordinate with product and frontend |

## Decision authority

Product scope and priorities: PO. Process: SM facilitates team agreement. Cross-component architecture: TL coordinates review with affected owners; product impact requires PO review. Specialist decisions: relevant owner plus qualified peer review. Technical implementation: developers collaborate. Critical security decisions require Security Owner involvement; do not let a single person approve their own design. Testing is shared; Backend Engineer coordinates product testing strategy, AI & Research Lead coordinates research evaluation, and independent reviewers verify critical evidence. These are proposed responsibility rules, not appointments.
